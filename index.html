The Opportunity

The Door Is Open in Chicago

Advocate's community partner forum in Chicago, hosted at Microsoft, gave Illinois partners a first real look at what AI can do. Dr. Oglesby-Odom's vision takes that across the enterprise.

Inside the walls, Work Forward is already equipping Advocate's workforce. The organizations Advocate funds and partners with, from community nonprofits to the FQHCs that carry outpatient care, need their own on-ramp. This concept builds it on two tracks, one model for every market.

One Model, Every Market

One Partner's Path

Every partner in every market moves through the same four steps. Scroll to follow one.

Show Up

Illustrative partner. Readiness scores come from each partner's check.

Step 1

Show Up

A hands-on session at the market's community listening event. Partners see AI do their kind of work, with their kind of documents.

  • Where listening sessions partners already attend
  • What live demonstrations on real community work
  • Who executive directors and the staff who do the work
Step 2

Check In

A short readiness check for every partner organization: people first, then process, then technology. Ten minutes, and each partner knows exactly where to start.

  • People comfort, skills, and champions on staff
  • Process where the hours go each week
  • Technology tools already in place to build on
Step 3

Put It to Work

A follow-up track where partners leave with tools working on their own tasks. Tool-agnostic: Microsoft, Google, or whatever each partner already runs.

  • Small Cohorts partners with similar work learn together
  • Real Tasks grants, reports, notes, outreach
  • Safe Use the traffic light rules built in from day one
Step 4

Report Back

Every partner's progress rolls up by market into one view for Advocate. Area VPs see where partners stand, and what works travels to the next market.

  • By Market where every partner stands today
  • Across the Footprint one view for enterprise leadership
  • Best Practices shared with the national health equity center

The FQHC Track
